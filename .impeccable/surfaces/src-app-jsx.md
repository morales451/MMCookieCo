# Surface brief: home page

Scope: the single-page home (`src/App.jsx` and its sections). Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: event and corporate buyers first (Cookie Nachos), Houston celebration buyers second (cookie cakes), weighted so neither crowds out the other.
Action: request a Cookie Nachos quote (headcount + date) or a cookie cake; both prefill the Netlify order-request form. Wholesale inquiries go to /wholesale.
Proof: six real testimonials; the owner's illustrated logo. No real product photography yet; every baked good is drawn.
Constraints: Cookie Nachos flavors are undecided (say they rotate) and pricing is quoted by headcount until the owner sets a per-pack price. Keep every section, form, and Netlify form name. The logo stays.
Memorable moments: chips around a bowl of buttercream dip themselves one by one; a typed message pipes onto a cookie cake.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the bakery's table seen from directly above. It opens on a tray of Cookie Nachos fanned around a bowl of buttercream dip, with the quote form set inside the dip, and it refuses the category default of a cream boutique page with a centered logo and a row of product cards.

OWN-WORLD: Strawberry-buttercream pink fields, baked-dough gold, and dark chocolate ink, with vanilla buttercream for dip and piping and sprinkle confetti only on sprinkles. A plump, heavy condensed grotesk carries headlines and copy, and glossy piped script is used for messages and names. Star-tip rosettes edge the sections, and every chip, cookie, cake, and pack is drawn from above in one light.

STORY: Visitors see Cookie Nachos at once and grasp the idea: chips and dip, one pack per guest, for their event. They enter a headcount and date and send a prefilled quote request. Cookie cakes follow as the second offer, with a live piped message.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Pink ground. A huge bowl of vanilla buttercream sits centered, ringed in raspberry, wider than the phone on mobile. Cookie chips fan around it, inner tips resting in the dip, and they dip themselves over time. Inside the dip: the "Cookie Nachos" headline, a one-line explanation, the headcount and date fields, and the "Get a Cookie Nachos quote" button.

FORM: Grounded candidate 3 of 7 (top-down bakery table), seed key 135d111c, re-centered on Cookie Nachos at the owner's request. Raised by declined challengers. From the indoor sun: each section commits to one flooded field color. From the sneaker box: one strict label grammar (term, then value or price) rules every product entry. From the cathode stack: every choice is visible at once and the selection snaps forward.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
