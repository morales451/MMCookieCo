# Surface brief: home page

Scope: the single-page home (`src/App.jsx` and its sections). Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: Houston celebration buyers and corporate/wholesale buyers, equally.
Action: request a cookie cake or dozen (Netlify order-request form); wholesale inquiries go to /wholesale.
Proof: six real testimonials; the owner's own illustrated logo. Product photos were stock; the redesign replaces them with authored cookie illustrations.
Constraints: keep every section, form, and Netlify form name. The logo stays. The user granted full creative liberty and lifted the old cream/mauve look as a commitment.
Memorable moment: you type your message and it pipes onto a giant cookie cake in buttercream.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a cookie cake seen from directly above, and the visitor's own message is the headline, piped in buttercream as they type. It refuses the category default: a cream boutique page with a serif title, a centered logo, and a row of product cards.

OWN-WORLD: Strawberry-buttercream pink fields, baked-dough gold, and dark chocolate ink, with vanilla buttercream for piping and sprinkle confetti (sky, lemon, mint, tangerine) only on sprinkles. Glossy piped script lettering for messages and names, set against a plump, heavy grotesk for headlines and copy. Star-tip rosettes form borders and section edges. Cookies are drawn from above, at scale.

STORY: Visitors see the product at once, write their own message on it, and understand the offer: sizes, flavors, prices, and how pickup works. Real testimonials make them believe it, and they send a request already prefilled with their cake.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Pink ground. A cookie cake wider than the viewport is tall sits centered, cropped top and bottom, with its rosette rim arcing in from the sides. A chocolate headline sits on the dough above the piped message. The live message is piped across the middle at roughly 9vw. Below it on the dough sits the composer: a message input, frosting dollops, size choice, and the primary "Request this cake" button.

FORM: Grounded candidate 3 of 7 (top-down piped cookie cake). Seed key 135d111c. Raised by declined challengers. From the indoor sun: each section commits to one flooded field color. From the sneaker box: one strict label grammar (name, notes, price) rules every product entry. From the cathode stack: every choice is visible at once and the selection snaps forward.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
